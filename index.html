Belloop Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Belloop is a pantry app made by a team of students at the Apple Developer Academy. We wanted it to work without knowing anything about you, so we don't have servers, accounts or analytics. This page explains what the app does with your data, in plain words.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't collect, store or sell any personal data, and we can't see what you put in the app.

Where your pantry is saved

  • Your products, expiry dates and photos are saved on your iPhone.
  • If you're signed in to iCloud, they're also saved in your own private iCloud, so they stay the same on all your devices. Only you can see that data. We can't.
  • Your name, username, profile picture, country and settings stay on your iPhone.

Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes and expiry dates. The image is processed on your iPhone and is never uploaded or saved, unless you choose to take a photo of a product, which stays in your pantry.

Services the app talks to

To find product names and recipes, the app sends a few requests to two free public databases:

  • Open Food Facts receives the barcode you scan or the product name you type.
  • TheMealDB receives the ingredient names used to look for recipes.

These requests don't include your name or anything that identifies you, but like any website, these services can see your IP address. You can read their own privacy policies on their sites.

Translations and ingredient matching are done on your iPhone with Apple's built-in frameworks, so that text doesn't leave the device.

Notifications

Reminders about products that are about to expire are created on your iPhone. They don't go through any server.

Tracking and ads

There's no tracking, no ads and no third-party analytics.

Deleting your data

Delete a product in the app and it's gone, from your iCloud too. If you delete the app, everything on the iPhone is removed. To also remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Belloop.

Changes

If we change how the app uses data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
